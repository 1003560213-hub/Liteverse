## Generated 3D universe assets

Every file under `public/universe/` is project-generated and distributed under
the repository's MIT License:

- `public/universe/manifest.json`
- `public/universe/galaxies/g01-grand-design-spiral.lvpc` through
  `g10-seyfert-spiral.lvpc`
- `public/universe/deep-field.lvpc`

The files are generated deterministically by
`tools/blender/build_universe_assets.py` (project code, MIT) using Blender 4.5
LTS and numpy. The inputs are procedural models and a seed, recorded in the
manifest together with the script SHA-256. No third-party textures, HDRIs,
photographs, survey catalogues or observatory imagery are used.

The Cycles review renders in `tools/blender/previews/` are generated by the
same script and are also project-generated under the MIT License.
